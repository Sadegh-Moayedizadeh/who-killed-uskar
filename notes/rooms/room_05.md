# Room 5

## Label

How does magic work?

## Description

The Altar of Mages. A grand, sacred school of magical arts — the central hub of the tutorial phase. The chamber is vast and deliberately institutional: high vaulted ceilings, stone pillars inscribed with layered magical notation, and the ambient presence of other mages moving through their own work. Everything here has been arranged for teaching. It is a place that takes its purpose seriously.

The player arrives here as Bol, a known apprentice mage, and is recognized as such. The room does not treat the possession of Bol by an Outer God as a crisis. Sekmidorgue, Bol's new mentor, regards it as a sacred development — rare, and worth careful instruction.

Five doors lead outward from this room into dedicated tutorial chambers, one for each discipline Sekmidorgue considers essential. A sixth door, Door 14, stands locked at the far end of the room, leading deeper into the game. It does not open until all five disciplines have been demonstrated and the Purified Chalice has been returned.

## Contents

### Creatures

**Sekmidorgue**

A high mage. Stern but fair. After Bournache's sacrifice, Sekmidorgue becomes Bol's new mentor, and this is their first formal session together. Sekmidorgue knows that Bol is now guided by an Outer God and treats this not as a contamination but as a holy circumstance — unusual, demanding, and worthy of respect.

Sekmidorgue runs this room. He greets Bol upon arrival, explains the sacred nature of what has occurred, and lays out the five lessons. Before the player enters each tutorial room, Sekmidorgue provides a briefing and the relevant starting artifact. After the player returns from each room, Sekmidorgue debriefs them. When all five rooms have been completed and the Purified Chalice is presented, he unlocks Door 14 and dismisses Bol forward.

**Other unnamed mages**

Present in the room. They acknowledge Bol as a known apprentice but do not engage meaningfully. If the player approaches any of them, the response is consistent: "I am not your mentor, Bol. Return to Sekmidorgue." They cannot be interacted with beyond this redirection at this stage of the game.

### Doors

**Door 8 — from Room 4 (entry)**

Two-way. No time change. The door through which the player arrives from Room 4 as Bol.

**Door 9 — to Room 6**

Two-way. No time change. Leads to the combat and soul-purification tutorial: a chamber with chained monsters the player must fight to demonstrate soul mechanics in practice. Sekmidorgue unlocks this door and instructs the player to enter when assigning the first lesson.

**Door 10 — to Room 7**

Two-way. No time change. Leads to the art gallery — a tutorial on magical paintings and how to read and interact with them.

**Door 11 — to Room 8**

Two-way. No time change. Leads to the scriptorium — a tutorial on notes, written records, and how they function as mechanical objects in the game world.

**Door 12 — to Room 9**

Two-way. No time change. Leads to the armory — a tutorial on magical weapons, their properties, and their relationship to soul state.

**Door 13 — to Room 10**

Two-way. No time change. Leads to the crystalline vault — a tutorial on temporal locks and time manipulation mechanics.

**Door 14 — forward, locked**

Leads deeper into the game beyond the tutorial phase. Locked on arrival and throughout the five lessons. Unlocked by Sekmidorgue only after the player has completed all five tutorial rooms and returned the Purified Chalice from Room 10. Passage through Door 14 increments time by +1.

## Entry Flows

### Regular Entry

The player enters Room 5 as Bol via Door 8 from Room 4. This is the first time the player has controlled Bol. The soul carries whatever state it held before possession, which at this point is pure.

Sekmidorgue is waiting. He greets Bol by name and addresses the possession directly: an Outer God now guides this apprentice, which is a rare and holy thing. Sekmidorgue does not dwell on the loss of Bournache — he acknowledges it briefly and moves on. The session is what matters now.

Sekmidorgue then assigns five lessons, each corresponding to one of the tutorial rooms. He explains the purpose of each room in broad terms, provides the starting artifact relevant to each lesson, and opens Door 9 as the first suggested entry point. The player may complete the five rooms in any order; Sekmidorgue responds appropriately whether the player follows the suggested sequence or deviates from it.

Other mages in the room are visible and moveable-toward but will not engage meaningfully. Any attempt to interact with them returns the same redirect.

### Completing the Tutorial Phase

After the player has completed all five tutorial rooms — Rooms 6 through 10 — and has returned the Purified Chalice from Room 10, they present it to Sekmidorgue. Sekmidorgue examines it, confirms it is purified, and delivers a short debrief: the five disciplines are now demonstrated, the Outer God has shown it can work through Bol with competence, and what lies ahead will require everything learned here.

He then unlocks Door 14. The player passes through. Time increments by +1.
