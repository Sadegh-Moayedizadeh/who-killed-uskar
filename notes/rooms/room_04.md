# Room 4

## Label

Who am I?

## Description

A quiet, dim chamber that feels designed rather than discovered. The walls are smooth and unadorned. There is no visible door. There is no weapon. There is nothing violent about the room at all — it is a room built for a single purpose, and that purpose is to strip the player of the vessel they arrived in.

This is the revelation room. The label is the question the player has been moving toward since the beginning, though they could not have known it. By the time they leave, the question is answered: the player is not the character they have been playing. They are the Outer God who has been controlling vessels in this world. The character — the unnamed original person — was never the point.

The room was designed by Bournache. Every element of it is intentional. Bol is here because Bournache sent him here before his sacrifice, with specific instructions and a specific vial.

## Contents

### Creatures

**Bol**

A young apprentice mage. Kind, earnest, and somewhat naive. He was sent here by Bournache before Bournache sacrificed himself — given instructions and the poison nectar, and told what to say and when. Whether Bol knows the nectar is poison, or knows only that drinking it will make a door visible, is ambiguous. He does not lie. He does not commit a sin. What he says is technically true: drinking the nectar will make the door visible. He presents himself as a guide and offers the nectar openly, without pressure or deception in his own mind. He genuinely believes he is helping.

Bol cannot be killed in this room. The player arrived here without weapons — Door 5 from Room 3 filters them out — so no weapon exists to use against him.

### Artifacts

**Poison Nectar**

A small ornate vial held by Bol and offered to the player's character. Drinking it kills the original vessel. Bol tells the player truthfully that drinking it will make the door visible. He is correct: the door becomes visible once the player, freed from the original vessel, inhabits Bol and perceives the room through Bol's properties as a known mage.

### Doors

**Door 8 — Divine Door to Room 5**

Leads to Room 5 (The Altar of Mages). One-way. Increments time by +1 on passage.

This door is invisible to the original unnamed vessel. It is visible only to a creature possessing the properties of a known mage. Bol qualifies. The original vessel does not. The door also requires a pure soul and no weapons — both of which Bol satisfies, and both of which the player already satisfies by virtue of having entered this room.

The door becomes visible to the player only after they have inhabited Bol, because it is only through Bol that they can perceive it.

## Entry Flows

### Regular Entry

The player enters Room 4 from Room 3 via Door 5 in the original unnamed vessel. The soul is pure (purified by Bournache's sacrifice). There are no weapons.

The room has no visible door. Bol is present and approaches. He introduces himself as a guide sent to help. He offers the poison nectar and tells the player that drinking it will make the door visible.

When the player's character drinks the nectar, the original vessel dies.

The game pauses. The player is shown a list of all known creatures with souls:

- **The unnamed original character** — DEAD
- **Lisspeg** — DEAD (killed by the player in Room 1)
- **Bournache** — DEAD (sacrificed himself in Room 3)
- **Bol** — ALIVE

The only living soul available to inhabit is Bol. The player takes control of Bol.

Now controlling Bol — a known mage with a pure soul and no weapons — Door 8 becomes visible for the first time. The player, as Bol, passes through Door 8 into Room 5. Time increments by +1.

The label pays off here. "Who am I?" is answered not by dialogue or revelation text but by mechanics: the player is removed from the character they have inhabited since T=0, shown the full ledger of souls, and forced to choose a new body. They are the one choosing. They were always the one choosing. They are the Outer God.

### Alternate Paths

**If the player refuses the nectar:**

The room has no exit for the original vessel. There is no door visible to them, no weapon, and no other creature to interact with. The player is stuck. The only way forward is to drink the nectar. There is no alternate resolution at this stage of the game.

**Late-game possibility — time manipulation:**

A player returning with deep knowledge of the timeline might attempt to bring a different known creature to this room through time manipulation. If a living soul with mage properties could be present here and the player inhabits them, Door 8 might become accessible by a different route. The full implications of this are not yet defined.
