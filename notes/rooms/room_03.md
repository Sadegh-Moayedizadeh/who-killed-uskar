# Room 3

## Label
What knowledge is more accurate?

## Description
A grand, multi-level library. Tall shelves of books line every wall, staircases connect upper and lower floors, and second-floor balconies overlook the central hall. The space is magnificent and overwhelming — the kind of place that feels designed to impress before it educates.

This is exactly what `Silarmousch` intended the player to walk into. The demonic path from `Room 2` leads here, and the room is the first full staging ground of that path. The player arrives already carrying the weight of `Lisspeg`'s murder. Their soul is weakened. `Door 6` — the demonic continuation — is visible to them. `Door 5` — the divine path — is not.

But the room is not wholly `Silarmousch`'s. `Bournache`, a divine mage, has been here too. He discovered what `Silarmousch` was doing with this place and altered it through a magical painting. His note sits prominently on a central reading stand, glowing and unmissable. His painting hangs on the wall, ready to be used. For any player who reads the note and understands its contents, `Room 3` becomes a crossroads: between `Silarmousch`'s demonic intentions and `Bournache`'s offer of purification.

The label hangs in the air. The library is full of answers. The question is which ones the player reaches for.

## Contents

### Creatures

#### Bournache
A divine mage. He does not appear on first entry. He arrives at T+1 relative to the player's first entry into this room — but only after the player has used the painting `The Great Dawn` to signal him.

`Bournache` is willing to sacrifice his soul to purify the player's corrupted soul. If the player accepts his sacrifice, he dies, and the purification is complete: `Door 5` becomes visible, `Door 6` disappears, and the divine path opens.

The player has three options when `Bournache` appears:

- **Accept the sacrifice.** `Bournache` dies willingly. The player's soul is purified. The divine path forward opens.
- **Kill him.** A deliberate second murder. The player's soul is driven deeper into corruption. This is the darkest available branch at this stage of the game.
- **Ignore him and continue.** The player stays on `Silarmousch`'s path without addressing `Bournache` at all. `Bournache`'s fate in this case is TBD.

#### Bournache's Note
A glowing parchment on the central reading stand. Visually unmissable — placed so that any player entering the room will notice it. It explains: the mechanics of soul soundness and how it governs door visibility, `Silarmousch`'s true intentions and the trap the player has already walked into, and how to use the painting `The Great Dawn` to contact `Bournache`. Signed by `Bournache`.

This note is designed so that the vast majority of players read it before doing anything else in the room.

#### The Great Dawn (Painting)
A magical painting hanging in the library. Using it at the appropriate time signals `Bournache` and causes him to appear in the room one time increment later. See **"The Art of Magic Paintings"** and **"Sinali the Painter-Mage"** for the mechanics behind it.

### Doors

#### Door 5 — Divine Door
Leads to `Room 4`. Requires a pure soul to become visible. On the player's initial arrival — soul corrupted by murder — this door is completely invisible.

It becomes visible only after the player's soul is fully purified, which at this point in the game requires accepting `Bournache`'s willing sacrifice. One-way.

#### Door 6 — Demonic Door
Continues `Silarmousch`'s dark path. Visible to the player on arrival due to their weakened soul. Leads to a darker branch of the game (destination TBD). One-way.

#### Door 7 — Return Door (Demonic, Two-Way)
Leads back to `Room 2`. Accessible regardless of soul state. Two-way. The player can use this door to advance time: travel to `Room 2` via `Door 7`, then return to `Room 3` via `Door 3`, incrementing time by one unit per round trip. This is the intended method for advancing to T+1 after using the painting.

## Entry Flows

### Regular Entry
The player enters `Room 3` from `Room 2` via `Door 3` (the Hidden Demonic Door), soul weakened by the murder of `Lisspeg`. On arrival, `Door 6` is visible and glowing. `Door 5` is invisible.

The player notices `Bournache`'s Note on the central reading stand. They read it. They learn the mechanics of soul soundness and door visibility, the truth of `Silarmousch`'s manipulation, and the method for contacting `Bournache` via the painting.

The player uses `The Great Dawn`. Nothing happens immediately — `Bournache` will appear at T+1.

To advance time by one unit, the player uses `Door 7` to return to `Room 2`, then re-enters `Room 3` via `Door 3`. Time is now T+1. `Bournache` appears.

The player accepts `Bournache`'s willing sacrifice. `Bournache` dies. The player's soul is purified. `Door 6` disappears. `Door 5` becomes visible for the first time. The player passes through `Door 5` into `Room 4`.

### Alternate Paths

**Demonic continuation (ignore Bournache's offer):** The player reads the note but chooses `Silarmousch`'s path anyway, passing through `Door 6` without using the painting or contacting `Bournache`. `Bournache` is never summoned. The player descends further into the demonic branch.

**Deepest corruption (kill Bournache):** The player contacts `Bournache`, waits for him to appear, and murders him instead of accepting the sacrifice. A second deliberate murder. The player's soul is driven deeper into corruption. This is the darkest branch available at this point in the game. `Door 6` remains the only forward path.

**Blind demonic path (skip the note):** A small fraction of players may enter `Room 3`, ignore `Bournache`'s Note entirely, and walk directly through `Door 6`. They proceed on `Silarmousch`'s path without ever learning the mechanics of soul soundness or knowing that `Bournache`'s offer existed.

**Return visits:** On any subsequent visit with a still-corrupted soul, `Door 6` remains visible and `Door 5` does not. If the player returns at a later point in the game with a sufficiently purified soul obtained by other means (monster-slaying or other received sacrifices), `Door 5` may become visible without needing `Bournache`'s sacrifice — though `Bournache` himself would already be gone.
