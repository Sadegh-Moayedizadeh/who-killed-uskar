# Lisspeg — First Meeting

- **Participants:** Lisspeg, the player (original vessel).
- **Room / Time:** Room 1, T=0.
- **Trigger:** The player enters Room 1 as the unnamed original vessel. Lisspeg is the only creature present and reacts the moment they appear.
- **Tone:** Warm, simple, overcome. Lisspeg is not performing devotion — he is experiencing it. He is not smart enough to be suspicious and not selfish enough to ask for anything. His joy is the most uncomplicated thing in the room.

See [story/characters.md](story/characters.md) for Lisspeg's profile and [rooms/room_01.md](rooms/room_01.md) for the room.

---

*Lisspeg looks up the moment the player enters. His face breaks into an expression of staggered relief — the kind that only comes after a very long wait.*

**LISSPEG:**
You came.

*He steps forward, hands clasped, eyes full.*

**LISSPEG:**
You actually came. I have waited so long in this room. I never doubted — not once, not for a single day — but still. To see it. To see you standing there.

*He steadies himself with a slow breath.*

**LISSPEG:**
Silarmousch the mage came to me in a dream. A real dream — the kind that doesn't fade when you wake, the kind that stays with you like a second skin. He told me to come to this chamber and wait. He said a prophet would arrive. Someone chosen. Someone who would carry me forward — toward the gods. He gave me this key and said: give it to the one who comes through that door.

*He produces the key — Silarmousch's Ward Key — and holds it out with both hands, as if offering something sacred.*

**LISSPEG:**
That is you. I know it is you.

*He waits. He is not testing the player. He simply believes.*

---

### Player Choice: Accept the key

**LISSPEG:**
Yes. Take it. It was always meant for you.

*He releases the key without any hesitation.*

**LISSPEG:**
The door at the far end — that key will open it. Beyond it, I'm told, is the way forward. I don't know more than that. Silarmousch said what was necessary and no more. But I trust it. I've trusted it all this time.

*He smiles.*

**LISSPEG:**
I am glad I waited.

---

## Optional Questions

*The player may ask optional questions before or after taking the key. Lisspeg answers each one without any change to his warmth or willingness.*

---

**[PLAYER CHOICE] "Who is Uskar?"**

*Lisspeg pauses. A small crease forms across his brow — not suspicion, just the effort of reaching for something half-heard.*

**LISSPEG:**
Uskar. I've heard that name. Somewhere. Someone said it once, a long time ago — I couldn't tell you who now. There was something sad about how they said it. Like someone who was lost.

*He shakes his head slowly.*

**LISSPEG:**
I don't know more than that. I'm sorry. Is Uskar someone you're looking for?

*He waits, genuinely hoping the player will tell him. He cannot be told anything that would disturb him.*

---

**[PLAYER CHOICE] "Who is Silarmousch?"**

*Lisspeg's whole face changes — not dramatically, but visibly. Something in him settles into reverence.*

**LISSPEG:**
A great mage. One of the greatest living, they say. He came to me in that dream and I knew — I just knew — that he was a figure of real power. Not the kind that shouts. The kind that doesn't need to.

*He leans forward slightly, confiding.*

**LISSPEG:**
He was kind to me. He explained everything calmly. He told me what was coming, what to do, what the key was for. Some people would have been frightened by a dream like that. I felt chosen. I felt like the luckiest creature alive.

*A beat.*

**LISSPEG:**
Is he known to you?

---

**[PLAYER CHOICE] "What is beyond the door?"**

*Lisspeg looks at the door — Door 1 — with the slight squint of a creature thinking carefully about something they actually know very little about.*

**LISSPEG:**
Forward. That's all I know. Silarmousch said it leads forward and that was enough for me. I didn't ask for a map.

*A small, self-deprecating laugh.*

**LISSPEG:**
Maybe I should have asked more questions. But I've found that when something feels true all the way down in your bones, asking too many questions just gets in the way.

---

*After any or all optional questions, Lisspeg returns to his quiet, waiting contentment. He has done what he was here to do. He has no further agenda and asks for nothing.*
