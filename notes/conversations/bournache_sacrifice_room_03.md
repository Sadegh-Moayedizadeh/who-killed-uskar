# Bournache — The Sacrifice

- **Participants:** Bournache, the player (original vessel).
- **Room / Time:** Room 3, T+1.
- **Trigger:** The player has used The Great Dawn painting and advanced time by one unit (via Door 7 to Room 2 and back through Door 3). Bournache is now present in the room, at the central stand, calm — as if he has been waiting, which, in a sense, he has.
- **Tone:** Steady, warm without being soft. Bournache has made his decision and made peace with it. He is not trying to comfort the player — he is being honest with them. There is a clarity about him that comes from having already settled the hard questions before the conversation began.

See [story/characters.md](story/characters.md) for Bournache's profile and [rooms/room_03.md](rooms/room_03.md) for the room. This conversation follows [bournache_note_room_03.md](bournache_note_room_03.md).

---

*Bournache stands at the central stand, one hand resting on the edge of it, facing the player. He does not move forward to close the distance. He simply is there, exactly as he said he would be.*

**BOURNACHE:**
You used the painting. I'm glad.

*He looks at the player carefully — not calculating, just present.*

**BOURNACHE:**
I know what you are. I have known since before you arrived in that first room. What is inhabiting you is older and larger than a creature, and I will not pretend otherwise. What you have been through in these first rooms — that was not an accident, and it was not your fault. You walked into something that was built to catch you.

*A beat.*

**BOURNACHE:**
You read the note. Then you know what I am offering and why. I have had a long time to consider this. It is not a sudden decision. I made it when I understood what Silarmousch had done here and what it would cost to undo it.

*He meets the player's gaze directly.*

**BOURNACHE:**
I am asking: do you accept?

---

### Player Choice: Accept the Sacrifice

**BOURNACHE:**
Then we are done deliberating.

*He steps slightly closer. His voice does not drop in volume — it just becomes quieter in quality, if that is possible.*

**BOURNACHE:**
I want you to understand one thing before I do this. Not for my sake — I am at peace — but because it matters for what comes after.

*He pauses.*

**BOURNACHE:**
Silarmousch designed this path because he knows something about what arrives in that first room. He knows you are coming and he has reasons for wanting you corrupted. I don't know all of those reasons yet. You will need to find them.

*He straightens.*

**BOURNACHE:**
Carry this forward. That is all I ask.

*He closes his eyes. The sacrifice begins — not violently. There is a shift in the air, a kind of stillness, and then the purification moves through the original vessel like a tide going out. When it is done, Bournache is gone. Door 6 has disappeared. On the far wall, for the first time, a door is visible that was not there before.*

---

### Player Choice: Refuse

*A brief pause. Bournache does not flinch or argue.*

**BOURNACHE:**
All right. That is your choice to make.

*He looks at the room around them — the central stand, the still-visible Door 6.*

**BOURNACHE:**
If you change your mind, use the painting again. I'll come back. I have time enough for that.

*He waits. If the player uses the painting again and advances time, the scene restarts from his arrival.*

---

## Optional Questions

---

**[PLAYER CHOICE] "Why are you doing this?"**

*Bournache considers how to answer — not because he doesn't know, but because he is choosing how much of the truth to give.*

**BOURNACHE:**
Because I believe the work matters.

*He pauses, then adds:*

**BOURNACHE:**
I have spent a long time watching what Silarmousch does and what it costs. He is very skilled at making harm look like guidance. There is no one else here who can counter what he built in these rooms — not in the way that's actually needed. If I can purify your soul and put you on the right path, that is worth more than whatever I would do with the rest of my years.

*He is not being self-pitying. He states it the way you would state a calculation.*

**BOURNACHE:**
I also believe that what you are carries the possibility of understanding something important about this world. Something that needs to be understood. I want to be part of how that becomes possible.

---

**[PLAYER CHOICE] "What happens to you?"**

*Bournache looks down for just a moment, then back up.*

**BOURNACHE:**
I will cease. The body goes. But a soul that is freely given does not disappear — it passes into whoever receives it. Something of me will travel with you. I find that more comforting than I expected.

*The faintest suggestion of a smile.*

**BOURNACHE:**
I am not frightened. I have been a mage long enough to understand what souls are and what they do. Mine has been a good one. I would rather it go somewhere useful than spend out quietly.

---

**[PLAYER CHOICE] "Who killed Uskar?"**

*For the first time, Bournache goes still. Not frozen — just paused, as though the question moved something in him that isn't usually moved.*

**BOURNACHE:**
I don't know the answer.

*He lets that sit for a moment before continuing.*

**BOURNACHE:**
I know the name. I know the weight of what that death caused. I know that it is not a simple story — there are too many forces arranged around it for it to be simple. But I do not know who did it, or precisely why, or what it means for everything that has followed.

*He looks at the player with something that might be called certainty, though it carries no arrogance.*

**BOURNACHE:**
I believe you will find it. That is part of why I'm here. You are going somewhere I cannot follow. The least I can do is make sure you get there.
