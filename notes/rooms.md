# Room 1

## label
Who killed Uskerr?

## Description
The starting point of the physical game. It is a seemingly simple, enclosed space that serves as the player's introduction to their initial, unnamed vessel and the basic mechanics of time and interaction.

## Contents

### Creatures

#### Lisspeg
A simple creature seeking truth and a meeting with the gods. Guided by the mage `Silarmousch`. Believes the player is a promised prophet.

### Doors

#### Door 1
A locked door. Requires a specific key to pass. The key is given by `Lisspeg` after interacting with him. Passing through increments time by 1 unit. It gets you to `Room 2`.

## Regular entry flow
This is the default spawn location. The player enters this room when they initiate the game, possessing the unnamed original character.

# Room 2

## Label
What is the cost of progression?

## Description
A trap room designed by `Silarmousch` to force the player into committing their first cardinal sin (murder). It introduces the concepts of soul degradation, weapons, and hidden paths.

## Contents

### Artifacts

#### Sinner's dagger
A physical weapon placed here for the player to acquire.

### Doors

#### Door 2
A one-way return door that traps the player upon entering. Increments time by 1 unit. The point of this door is make the player to feel traped between `Room 1` and `Room 2`, getting back to kill `Lisspeg` at the start, and also pass time arbitrarily by the player by combining with `Door 1`.

#### Door 3
It's a hidden demonic door. Leads to `Room 3`. It has visibility constraint that requires a weakened/impure soul (achieved by killing Lisspeg).

#### Door 4
It's a hidden devine door. Its visibility constraint is that it requires a strong/pure soul (inaccessible at the first stages of the game). Later in the game the player can return and go this path.

## Regular entry flow
The player enters from `Room 1` after interacting with `Lisspeg` and using the acquired key to unlock the door. Then picks the artifact `Sinner's dagger`. Goes back to `Room 1`. Kills `Lisspeg`. Comes back. Then passes through `Door 3` that is now visible due to a weakened soul.

# Room 3

## Label
What knowledge is more accurate?

## Description
A grand, multi-level library filled with books and staircases. It represents a branching path between the intentions of two mages: `Silarmousch` (who wants the player to walk the demonic path) and `Bournache` (who offers a chance at divine purification).

## Contents

### Creatures

#### Bournache
Appears only if the player uses the painting and manipulates time correctly. He is willing to sacrifice his soul to the player.

### Artifacts

#### Book 1
Explains the concept of doors.

#### Book 2
Explains the concept of time and its subjectivity.

#### Book 3
Explains the concept of gods.

#### Book 4
Explains the concept of free will.

#### Book 5
Explains the joy of being posessed by a god.

#### Book 6
A mythical story about a creature named `Gounargoun` who was posessed by a god. He came on to do many greate deeds. Finally achieved nirvana.

#### Book 7
A rather horror story about a creature named `Zortaug` who chose the demonic path. Finally became a monster.

#### Book 8
A story about a creature name `Pyutkah` who saught to meet a god, went to many pilgrimages, but were unable to. He was always in regret for that and saught the destiniy of `Gounargoun`. Finally he sacrafices himself for a creature who was very similar to `Gounargoun` and was trying to pass through a devine door which originally could't.

#### Book 9
A story about a creature named `Daghal` who decieved everyone in his path including some pilgrims. He later aquired the art of morphing and changed objects at his will. He decieved other creatures to sacrafice themselves for him so that he could purify his soul and keep doing his evil deeds. His outcome is not known.

Explains about monsters.

#### Book 11
Explains about sacrafice and corresponding rituals.

#### Book 12

#### Book 13
Explains about the cardinal sin of deceit.

#### Book 14

#### Book 15

#### Book 17

#### Bounache's note
Highly visible, unmissable text explaining souls, demons, doors, and the mages' true intentions.

#### Painting "the great dawn"
Acts as a communication device to signal Bournache.

### Doors

#### Door 5
Leads to `Room 4`. Visibility constraint: Requires a pure soul (achieved via Bournache's sacrifice).

#### Door 6
A door continuing Silarmousch's dark path. Visible due to the player's weakened soul.

(There are more doors.)

## Regular entry flow
The player enters via the Hidden Demonic Door from `Room 2`, having committed the sin of killing `Lisspeg` to weaken their soul and reveal the path. The player may keep on going through the demonic path or read `Bournache` note and summon him to do the sacrafice and progress through the devine path. The player is also able to read books.
