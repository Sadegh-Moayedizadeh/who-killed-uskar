# Room 1

## label
Who killed Uskerr?

## Description
The starting point of the physical game. It is a seemingly simple, enclosed space that serves as the player's introduction to their initial, unnamed vessel and the basic mechanics of time and interaction.

## Contents

### Creatures

#### Lisspeg
A simple creature seeking truth and a meeting with the gods. Guided by the mage `Silarmousch`. Believes the player is a promised prophet.

### Doors

#### Door 1
A locked door. Requires a specific key to pass. The key is given by `Lisspeg` after interacting with him. Passing through increments time by 1 unit. It gets you to `Room 2`.

## Regular entry flow
This is the default spawn location. The player enters this room when they initiate the game, possessing the unnamed original character.

# Room 2

## Label
What is the cost of progression?

## Description
A trap room designed by `Silarmousch` to force the player into committing their first cardinal sin (murder). It introduces the concepts of soul degradation, weapons, and hidden paths.

## Contents

### Artifacts

#### Sinner's dagger
A physical weapon placed here for the player to acquire.

### Doors

#### Door 2
A one-way return door that traps the player upon entering. Increments time by 1 unit. The point of this door is make the player to feel traped between `Room 1` and `Room 2`, getting back to kill `Lisspeg` at the start, and also pass time arbitrarily by the player by combining with `Door 1`.

#### Door 3
It's a hidden demonic door. Leads to `Room 3`. It has visibility constraint that requires a weakened/impure soul (achieved by killing Lisspeg).

#### Door 4
It's a hidden devine door. Its visibility constraint is that it requires a strong/pure soul (inaccessible at the first stages of the game). Later in the game the player can return and go this path.

## Regular entry flow
The player enters from `Room 1` after interacting with `Lisspeg` and using the acquired key to unlock the door. Then picks the artifact `Sinner's dagger`. Goes back to `Room 1`. Kills `Lisspeg`. Comes back. Then passes through `Door 3` that is now visible due to a weakened soul.

# Room 4

## Contents

### Creatures

#### Bol
An apprentice mage sent by `Bournache`. He acts as a messenger and unknowingly serves as the player's next vessel.

### Artifacts

#### Poison nectar
A lethal drink offered by Bol.

### Doors

#### Door 7
A devine door that leads to `Room 5`. Its filter constraint is that the creature passing it should not carry any weapons. The creature also nneds to have a rather pure soul.

## Regular entry flow
The player enters through the Divine Door in `Room 3`, having accepted Bournache's soul sacrifice to purify their own soul. To progress out of this room, the current vessel must die via poison, and the player must assume control of Bol.

# Room 6

## Label
What becomes of the soulless?

## Description
A dimly lit, stone-walled annex located beneath the Altar of Mages. This room serves as a practical combat and soul-purification tutorial. It houses creatures that have completely destroyed their souls and become monsters. By slaying these monsters, the player learns the mechanics of stamina, blocking, and parrying, while actively purifying their vessel's (Bol's) soul to prepare for higher magical arts.

## Contents

### Creatures

#### Chained Monster (x2)
Former mages who committed too many cardinal sins and lost their souls entirely. They are chained to the walls, limiting their movement, making them ideal practice targets. They attack in slow, predictable patterns.

### Artifacts

#### Training Blade
A standard physical sword provided by Sekmidorgue. It deals low damage but consumes very little stamina, encouraging the player to practice chained hits and parries.

#### Wooden Shield
A basic shield for practicing blocks and parries.

### Doors

#### Door 8
A simple, two-way door that leads back to `Room 5`. It does not affect time.

## Regular entry flow
The player enters from `Room 5` after receiving instructions from `Sekmidorgue` to purify their soul. The player must equip the `Training Blade` and `Wooden Shield`, practice the combat mechanics to defeat the chained monsters, and return to `Room 5` with a strengthened soul.

# Room 7

## Label
Can the mind touch the distant?

## Description
An ornate, circular art gallery. This room is dedicated to teaching the player how to interact with and manipulate Magical Paintings. The room contains a puzzle where the physical environment is directly tied to the canvas.

## Contents

### Artifacts

#### Painting "The Locked Chest"
A magical painting depicting a small, bronze chest sitting in a sunlit meadow. The chest in the painting has a heavy iron lock on it. 

#### Physical Bronze Chest
A real chest sitting in the center of the room. It is completely indestructible and sealed shut by an iron lock identical to the one in the painting, but there is no physical key in the room.

#### Brush of Alteration
A magical artifact that allows a creature with a sufficiently pure soul to alter the contents of a magical painting.

#### Scroll of Insight
Inside the physical chest. It grants a deep understanding of remote manipulation and further purifies the soul upon reading.

### Doors

#### Door 9
A two-way door leading back to `Room 5`. Does not affect time.

## Regular entry flow
The player enters from `Room 5`. To solve the puzzle, the player must use the `Brush of Alteration` on the `Painting "The Locked Chest"` to erase or unlock the iron lock on the canvas. Because the painting is magical and tied to reality, altering the painting causes the lock on the `Physical Bronze Chest` in the room to click open, allowing the player to retrieve the `Scroll of Insight`.

# Room 8

## Label
What hides in plain sight?

## Description
A quiet, dusty scriptorium filled with floating parchment. This room teaches the mechanics of Notes—specifically how to decode them, alter them, and use them to reveal hidden truths about the environment.

## Contents

### Artifacts

#### Encoded Note "The Unseen Path"
A note left by a past mage. It appears as gibberish to the naked eye.

#### Cipher Lens
A magical magnifying glass. When held, it translates specific encoded notes if the player's soul is pure enough to handle the truth.

#### Alteration Quill
A magical quill that allows the player to rewrite specific keywords in a decrypted note, slightly changing the local reality.

### Doors

#### Door 10
A two-way door leading back to `Room 5`. Does not affect time.

## Regular entry flow
The player enters from `Room 5`. They must pick up the `Cipher Lens` to read the `Encoded Note`. The decrypted note reads: *"The lantern in the corner burns bright, blinding the way."* The player must then use the `Alteration Quill` to change the word *"bright"* to *"dark"*. This extinguishes the physical lantern in the room, casting a shadow that reveals the glowing exit sigil.

# Room 9

## Label
Does faith hold weight?

## Description
A grand armory lined with glowing weaponry. This room introduces the concept of Magical Weapons and how their effectiveness scales directly with the strength and purity of the user's soul.

## Contents

### Creatures

#### Armored Husk
An animated suit of thick armor powered by residual magic. It is completely immune to physical damage and demonic artifacts. 

### Artifacts

#### Blade of the Zealous
A magical sword. Its blade is dull and heavy in the hands of a sinner, but becomes razor-sharp and weightless in the hands of a pure soul. 

### Doors

#### Door 11
A two-way door leading back to `Room 5`. Does not affect time.

## Regular entry flow
The player enters from `Room 5`. If the player attempts to strike the `Armored Husk` with standard physical weapons, they will deal zero damage. The player must equip the `Blade of the Zealous`. Because the player (controlling Bol) has been purifying their soul in the previous tutorial rooms (especially Room 6), the magical blade scales with their high soul purity, allowing them to shatter the husk in a few strikes.

# Room 10

## Label
Can a moment be caged?

## Description
A crystalline vault where the air shimmers. This room acts as the final tutorial, teaching the complex mechanics of Temporal Locks—locking an item not just in physical space, but in a specific instance of time.

## Contents

### Artifacts

#### Chronos Lock
A specialized lock that binds an object to the exact integer of time it is currently in.

#### Temporal Key
The only key capable of opening the Chronos Lock.

#### The Purified Chalice
A sacred artifact required by Sekmidorgue to prove Bol's mastery of the tutorial trials.

### Doors

#### Door 12 (Time Door)
A one-way door inside the room that loops back into the center of the same room, but **increments time by 1 unit**.

#### Door 13
A two-way door leading back to `Room 5`. Does not affect time.

## Regular entry flow
The player enters from `Room 5` at Time = $T$. The `Purified Chalice` is sitting on a pedestal, but it is secured by the `Chronos Lock`. The player holds the `Temporal Key`. 
The puzzle: If the player simply unlocks the Chalice, the room's defensive magic will instantly destroy it. The player must use `Door 12` to increment time to $T+1$. Because the Chalice was locked to time $T$ via the Chronos Lock, it does not exist in $T+1$, bypassing the room's defensive magic trigger. The player can then safely alter the pedestal's trap, use a different door mechanic (or time-decrement mechanic introduced by Sekmidorgue) to return to $T$, and safely use the `Temporal Key` to claim the Chalice.
